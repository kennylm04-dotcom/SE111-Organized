Synopsis

After the death of his father, T'Challa returns to the African nation of Wakanda to take his rightful place as king. But when a powerful enemy suddenly reappears, T'Challa's mettle as king — and as Black Panther — gets tested when he is drawn into a conflict that puts the entire fate of Wakanda and the world at risk. Faced with treachery and danger, the young king must rally his allies and release the full power of Black Panther to defeat his foes and secure the safety of his people and their way of life.
